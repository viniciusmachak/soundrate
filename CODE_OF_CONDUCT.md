# Code of Conduct

🇧🇷 [Leia em português](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in Soundrate a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are just starting out
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews, and also when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to the maintainer using the contact listed on their GitHub profile ([viniciusmachak](https://github.com/viniciusmachak)). All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
